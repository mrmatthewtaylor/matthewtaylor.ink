Trip to the Lake District

Lake District Stone Terraces & Bay Elevations

Field survey documenting vernacular Cumbrian random-rubble slate masonry, dressed lintels, canted ground-floor bay windows, and stepped stone boundary walls—used as reference for multi-gabled streetscape orthographics.

Victorian Slate Gabled Villa

Primary source study of a two-storey vernacular stone villa featuring dressed sandstone mullions, a gabled porch canopy, asymmetrical slate gables, and heavy stone quoining.

Classical Portico & Moulded Door Surround

Detailed orthographic reference capturing a paneled timber double-door flanked by leaded sidelights, set within a heavy ashlar stone surround featuring a triangular pediment, dentil courses, and layered slate cladding.

Vernacular Timber-Framed Streetscape & Octagonal Turret

Urban contextual capture documenting exposed mock-Tudor timber framing, a slate-roofed corner turret with lead cap finial, dormer profiles, and ground-floor shopfront fascia proportions.

Edwardian Shop Entrance & Curved Canopy Hood

Architectural detail study showcasing a six-panel timber door with leaded transom lights, framed by a classic timber canopy hood, moulded corbel bracket, and ball finial ornament.

Free-Standing Stone Tower & Weather Vane Monument

Field photography of a miniature slate and ashlar masonry tower featuring corner pinnacles, dressed quoins, a plank timber door with strap hinges, and a wrought-iron weather vane.

Tudor-Arch Portal with Carved Heraldic Relief

Close-up study of a recessed four-centre Tudor arch door casing in dressed sandstone, featuring spandrel rosette reliefs, an upper crest coat-of-arms, and slate ashlar wall infill.

Classical Archway Portal & Foliated Spandrels

Primary reference capture of a heavy round-arched doorway set between ashlar piers, detailing carved acanthus spandrels, a keystone motif, and moulded timber double doors.

Pedimented Window Surround & Slate Field

Tonal detail study focusing on an ornate stone window surround with a exaggerated triangular-topped apron/hood configuration, set against coursed slate masonry and rusticated ashlar pilasters.

About this collection

A curated repository of primary source photography capturing regional Cumbrian vernacular stone masonry, classical fenestration proportions, ornate door surrounds, and historical streetscape compositions—serving as the direct observational foundation for custom vector illustrations and architectural elevations.

About This Project

This illustration series explores classical architectural language and craftsmanship through the concept of spoliation—reimagining historical elements, such as Mannerist volutes and Renaissance pediments, within new digital compositions.

By combining disparate motifs through precise draftsmanship, proportion, and geometry, the project honours traditional architectural syntax while expanding its creative boundaries beyond rigid replication.

All illustrations in this project are entirely human-created. No artificial intelligence was used to generate or synthesise any visual content.
