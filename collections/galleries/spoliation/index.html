Studies in Eclectic Spoliation and Modular Reconstruction

Grand Classical Country House Elevation

A sweeping symmetrical palace elevation crowned by corner domes, a central cupola, a grand pedimented portico with classical columns, and an extended wing of uniform pedimented windows.

Asymmetric Revivalist Façade

A complex multi-level composition contrasting a detailed central pedimented block with a symmetrical multi-pane tower featuring arched upper windows and quoin-trimmed corners.

Stacked Fenestration Tower

A vertical multi-tier elevation integrating an arched roofline with corner urns, an upper balcony window with a triangular pediment, and a lower multi-pane window unit.

Pedimented Pavilion Portal

A symmetrical classical pavilion front featuring a triangular pediment, central oculus window, dentil molding, and flanking pilasters raised on a low base platform.

Paneled Double-Door Entrance

A monumental entrance composition defined by heavy double doors with recessed square panels, a prominent horizontal entablature frieze, and flanking pilaster details.

About This Project

This illustration series explores classical architectural language and craftsmanship through the concept of spoliation—reimagining historical elements, such as Mannerist volutes and Renaissance pediments, within new digital compositions.

By combining disparate motifs through precise draftsmanship, proportion, and geometry, the project honours traditional architectural syntax while expanding its creative boundaries beyond rigid replication.

All illustrations in this project are entirely human-created. No artificial intelligence was used to generate or synthesise any visual content.
