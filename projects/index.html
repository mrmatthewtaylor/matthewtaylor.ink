About This Project

This illustration series explores classical architectural language and craftsmanship through the concept of spoliation—reimagining historical elements, such as Mannerist volutes and Renaissance pediments, within new digital compositions.

By combining disparate motifs through precise draftsmanship, proportion, and geometry, the project honours traditional architectural syntax while expanding its creative boundaries beyond rigid replication.

All illustrations in this project are entirely human-created. No artificial intelligence was used to generate or synthesise any visual content.
